Go Book Marketplace

Request Management System (RMS)

Project Scope

  • Designed a web-based Request Management System (RMS) focused on the Admin/Reviewer experience for handling and managing incoming requests.
  • Covered core workflows including request listing, detailed review screens, status management, and approval actions (Accept, Reject, Send Back).
  • Built data-heavy interfaces such as tables, filters, and dashboards optimized for quick scanning and efficient decision-making.
  • Designed structured read-only detail views for book and user information with clear visual hierarchy and actionable insights.
  • Established a scalable design system using grid layout, reusable components, and consistent spacing for desktop interfaces.

My Role

Owned the end-to-end UX design of the Admin panel, improving request handling through structured review screens and clear approval workflows.

Problem Statement

Books are essential, but access isn’t. Students struggle to afford academic books, readers often buy books they only read once, and selling old books is inconvenient and unstructured. Existing platforms either focus only on buying or lack trust in peer-to-peer transactions.
The Gap:There is no unified, trustworthy platform that supports-

  • Buying new/used books
  • Renting affordably
  • Selling easily

Project Goals

To design a unified book marketplace that breaks down barriers between readers and knowledge, allowing users to effortlessly buy, rent, and sell books.

Buy New & Old Books

Affordable access to knowledge

Curate a vast, reliable collection where users can discover and purchase both brand-new releases and carefully inspected pre-owned books with complete confidence.

Rent Books Easily

Flexible reading experience

Provide an affordable alternative for students and casual readers to rent books for short durations, ensuring knowledge is accessible without the commitment of ownership.

Sell Without Friction

Give books a second life

Enable a seamless, trustworthy selling experience where users can effortlessly list their read books, connect with eager buyers, or sell directly to verified vendors.

Key Insights

“I don’t want to buy expensive books I’ll only use once”

→ Strong demand for rental model

“Selling old books is a hassle”

→ Need for quick sell flow + price guidance

“I don’t trust random sellers”

→ Trust is a major barrier in C2C marketplaces

“Too many choices confuse me”

→ Need for clear decision-making UI

The Solution

A centralized, deeply unified book marketplace that bridges the gap between readers, students, and sellers—transforming a fragmented experience into a secure, accessible ecosystem.

1. Discover

A Centralized Unified Hub

Eliminated the need to jump between multiple unverified platforms. Users can seamlessly browse new arrivals and pre-owned collections in one beautifully organized space, backed by trusted sellers.

2. Enable

Flexible Rental Model

Empowered students and short-term readers with affordable access. Introduced dynamic per-day pricing based on book condition (new vs. old), ensuring complete pricing transparency.

3. Experience

Frictionless Selling

Removed the hassle of C2C negotiations. Users can now easily upload their books, get automatic price suggestions based on market insights, and reach a massive audience of eager readers instantly.

Typography

Noto Sans Bold

Used primarily for main headings (H1, H2) and critical focal points. Bold weight establishes a strong visual hierarchy, ensuring that key sections immediately capture user attention and guide them through the content layout.

Noto Sans Medium

Applied to subheadings, button labels, and important navigation elements. Medium weight provides the perfect balance—it stands out enough to indicate interactivity or secondary importance without overpowering the primary headings.

Noto Sans Regular

The foundation for all body copy, paragraphs, and descriptions. Regular weight ensures maximum legibility and reading comfort, reducing cognitive load when users read long descriptions or instructional text.
