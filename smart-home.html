Project Overview

The Government e-Marketplace project was a large-scale procurement and marketplace platform designed to streamline the buying and selling process between government organizations, departments, vendors, and service providers.

As a UI/UX Designer at Tata Consultancy Services (TCS) Interactive, I worked closely with Business Analysts, Product Owners, Developers, and Stakeholders to translate approved BRDs (Business Requirement Documents) into scalable, user-centric digital experiences.

Platform Focus

  • Simplifying procurement workflows
  • Improving operational transparency
  • Managing large-scale catalogs and configurations
  • Enabling efficient marketplace operations
  • Providing analytics and reporting for administrators and departments

My contribution included end-to-end UX thinking, information architecture, workflow optimization, dashboard design, and scalable enterprise UI systems.

Design Approach

The project involved highly complex enterprise workflows with multiple user roles such as Buyers, Vendors, Marketplace Admins, Government Departments, and Procurement Teams.

Key Challenge: Converting complex procurement logic into simple and intuitive user experiences while maintaining compliance with government workflows and approval systems.

01

Category Creation

UX Impact: Simplified complex taxonomy, reduced admin confusion, and improved discoverability.

Key Features: Parent-child relationships, tagging, search/filter, bulk upload.

Catalog Creation

Key Features: Metadata management, media upload, draft/publish workflows, approval system.
