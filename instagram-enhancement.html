Instagram UX Redesign • Mobile Experience

What if Instagram Reels helped users stay mindful instead of endlessly scrolling?

Instagram Reels are designed to maximize engagement, but the infinite feed often causes users to lose track of time. This redesign introduces awareness-driven features that encourage intentional content consumption while preserving engagement and platform growth.

The Problem

Users open Reels for a few minutes but often spend significantly longer than intended due to endless scrolling and hyper-personalized recommendations.

Problem Statement

  • Instagram Reels are super engaging — maybe too engaging. The endless scroll and constantly personalized videos make it really easy to lose track of time. A lot of people end up watching for way longer than they planned, which can mess with their sleep, focus, and overall mental health.
  • The challenge is to come up with design ideas that help people use Reels more mindfully — without completely hurting Instagram’s business, which relies on people watching, sharing, and engaging with content regularly.

Solution

  • Introduce a real-time usage timer in the Reels section to help users track how much time they spend watching Reels each day, with the timer resetting every 24 hours. Additionally, implement a Reels category filter to allow users to view content based on their selected interests, promoting more intentional and focused browsing.
  • Applying a streak rule based on the watching time of Reels by category is a powerful way to increase user engagement and retention.
  • streak 1 is given for >=5 Minutes in 24 hours
  • Encourage users to continuously watch Reels from specific categories for a set duration per day, building habits and improving time spent on the platform.

Design Process

01

Problem Identification (User Research)

  • Understanding user pain points regarding excessive content consumption.
  • Clearly defining the problem statement to focus on mindful usage.
02

Ideation (Solution Exploration)

  • Brainstorming multiple solutions to address endless scrolling.
  • Using design thinking methods like mind mapping for feature integration.
03

User Feedback (Validation)

  • Gathering feedback on initial concepts from target demographics.
  • Validating assumptions with real users to ensure the solution is practical.
  • Refining the concept to the most viable solution.
04

Wireframing

  • Sketching rough layouts to visualize feature placement.
  • Focusing on structure, layout, and content flow within the existing app ecosystem.
05

Prototyping

  • Creating high-fidelity interactive mockups.
  • Using Figma to design realistic user interactions and animations.
